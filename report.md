# Energy Consumption Report – Iranian Schools

## Introduction

This report analyzes a dataset on energy consumption in educational buildings across Iran. The dataset was collected by Norouzi et al. (2023) and published in *Data in Brief*. It contains over 1,700 records from schools in 14 provinces, with data on natural gas consumption, outdoor temperature, environmental impact indicators, and payable amounts in both IRR and USD. After cleaning, the dataset includes monthly gas consumption, temperature statistics, and cost data.

## Insight 1: Winter Peaks in Gas Consumption

Gas consumption is highest during winter months, particularly in December, January, and February. This pattern is consistent with increased heating demand during cold seasons. The seasonal bar chart clearly shows that winter accounts for the largest share of total consumption, followed by fall, spring, and summer.

## Insight 2: Strong Correlation Between Consumption and Cost

The scatter plot reveals a positive relationship between gas consumption and payable amount. As consumption increases, the cost rises proportionally. This confirms that gas usage is the primary driver of energy costs in these schools.

## Insight 3: Seasonal Trend in the 12-Month Chart

The 12-month line chart shows a clear seasonal pattern: consumption rises in the first and last months of the year (cold months) and drops in the middle months (warm months). This trend repeats annually, indicating a predictable cycle that can be used for budgeting and planning.

## Recommendation

Given the high gas consumption in winter, it is recommended that schools optimize their heating systems and improve building insulation. Simple measures such as sealing windows and doors, installing programmable thermostats, and scheduling heating based on occupancy can significantly reduce gas usage and lower costs.
